# 📊 Business Sales Performance Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the **Future Interns – Data Science & Analytics Internship (Task 1)**.

The objective of this project was to analyze business sales data and develop a professional, interactive Power BI dashboard that helps businesses understand their sales performance, identify revenue trends, discover top-performing products and categories, and support data-driven decision-making.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful insights through data cleaning, KPI analysis, and business storytelling.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze revenue trends over time
- Identify top-selling products
- Evaluate category-wise sales performance
- Compare regional sales performance
- Understand customer segment distribution
- Generate meaningful business insights
- Provide actionable business recommendations

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes the following business KPIs and visualizations:

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Quantity Sold
- Profit Margin

### Interactive Visualizations

- Sales by Region
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Monthly Profit Trend
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Segment
- Sales by Ship Mode
- Top Selling Products
- Sales by Sub-Category
- Interactive Region Filter (Slicer)

---

## 📊 Key Business Insights

- The **West Region** generated the highest sales revenue.
- **Technology** was the highest revenue-generating category.
- The **Consumer Segment** contributed the largest share of total sales.
- **Chairs** and **Phones** were among the top-selling sub-categories.
- **Standard Class** was the most frequently used shipping mode.
- Sales remained strong throughout the analysis period with noticeable seasonal trends.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations can help improve business performance:

- Increase marketing efforts in high-performing regions.
- Focus inventory planning on top-selling products.
- Expand sales strategies for high-revenue categories.
- Improve promotional campaigns in lower-performing regions.
- Continue monitoring monthly sales trends for seasonal planning.
- Optimize shipping operations to improve customer satisfaction.
